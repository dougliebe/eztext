Draft Assistant

Upload your player projections CSV once. It's reused every draft until you replace it.

CSV format

One row per player, with a header row. Recognized columns (case-insensitive):

  • Name (or Player)
  • Position (or Pos)
  • Team (optional but recommended, esp. for D/ST rows)
  • ProjectedPoints (or Proj, FPTS, Points)

Then open an ESPN fantasy draft room — the assistant panel appears automatically.
